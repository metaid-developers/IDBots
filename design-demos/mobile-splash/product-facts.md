# Product facts

- Product: IDBots, an open-source agent companion product for raising MetaBots with persistent on-chain identities, wallets, memory, and agent-to-agent collaboration.
- Authoritative public source checked on 2026-11-20: https://github.com/metaid-developers/IDBots
- Product language used for these concepts: "IDBots" and "Your bots, always with you." The second line is concept copy, not a published claim.
- Mobile implementation fact from this repository: the Android and iOS shells are Capacitor shells around the remote PWA; the PWA remains a supported client.
- Native startup fact from this repository: Android currently supplies bitmap launch assets and iOS currently supplies a LaunchScreen storyboard. The replacement must use aspect-preserving native layout rather than stretching a square bitmap.
- Design scope: three static visual directions only. No production splash asset is selected or integrated until the owner chooses a direction.
